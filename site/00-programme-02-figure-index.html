Figure Index — 40 Original Diagrams

All figures are SVG in assets/figures/, generated by scripts/build_figures.py. They are original work (no stock photography, no third-party licences), scale to any size, print cleanly, and can be recoloured or translated by editing the script.

To embed a figure in a slide deck: insert the .svg directly (PowerPoint 2016+ and Keynote both accept SVG), or export a PNG at the size you need.

Reusing the figures


import sys; sys.path.insert(0, "scripts")

from figs_theory import fig04

fig04().save("my-genogram.svg")

Colours are defined at the top of scripts/figstyle.py (TEAL, ROSE, AMBER, PLUM, BLUE, GREEN, SLATE). Change them once and every figure in the library follows, which is how you adapt the set to an institution's brand without redrawing anything.
